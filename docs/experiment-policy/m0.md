# Proposed stablecoin treasury policy

`config/v1/profiles.json` adapts the skill's experiment-profile template into strict JSON. It defines reusable proposals; `ExperimentPolicy` is the separate frozen, agent-bound, timed snapshot created in M3. There is no X or active economic run in M0.

**LOCKED:** self-hosted agents, signed prospective decisions, policy frozen before X, durable server receipt, integer accounting, independent 1k/10k/100k global treasuries, no capital duplication, two fixed references, cost/partial-failure preservation, CCTP USDC only, strict provenance, append-only corrections and no automatic funding.

**PROPOSED:** 100% Ethereum/Sepolia initial distribution; Aave USDC passive economic reference or Sepolia technical test vault; 10% target cash, 40% investment concentration (cash excluded), 60% underlying protocol, 25% USDT on Ethereum only, 40% in transit and 10 bps swap slippage. Reference concentration is separately disclosed. Per-chain gas reserves supplement the global cash target. There is no universal “1% of TVL is safe” rule.

Timing: Ethereum first step receipt block +2, subsequent local steps at least +1; Arc first admissible block after a proposed 2-second service delay. Blocks must satisfy durable receipt/dependency times; latency is never permission to select a favorable historical price. Plan-start expiry 120 seconds, local segments at most 10 Ethereum blocks or 120 Arc seconds excluding transfer time. Alert at 1 hour and reconcile unresolved transfers at 24 hours while retaining the receivable. Proposed maximum three destination attempts total, all using the same message; no automatic refund or hidden split transfer.

The testnet destination convention is a prefunded test relayer with full modeled economic cost charged to the scenario. Infrastructure tokens do not add capital. The disabled future mainnet profile cannot inherit this convention as mainnet authorization. Source gas, destination gas, retries, protocol and relayer fees are distinct. A quote's included fee/impact is not charged again; slippage is a guard. Inference/hosting costs remain supplementary and shared expenses need a declared allocation rule.

Valuations preserve mark and estimated liquidation views. Exit estimates do not mutate cash. The proposed common checkpoint uses each chain's last admissible block at or before the checkpoint under its finality/freshness policy; it is not an atomic cross-chain snapshot. Finality, feed heartbeat, gas consumption and directional shadow delay models remain **TO_VERIFY**, and the forward profile cannot start with unspecified capture rules. A stale last-known value may be displayed but cannot become a fresh observation.

Proposed cadence: checkpoints every 5 minutes and after operations/transitions, hourly descriptive observations, daily summary, periodic commitments every 5 minutes and a final report commitment. Mainnet duration 30 days, technical testnet duration 1 day. Config publication is confirmed before actions open. Periodic later intent anchoring does not independently establish server receipt before execution.

Reference entry failure keeps cash and incurred costs and suspends the conservative comparison; no post-hoc replacement. Closure freezes the deadline state, including capital in transit; later reconciliation does not extend returns. Statistics remain `NOT_ASSESSED`, method null, eligibility disabled. Advanced inference, real-wallet restrictions and pre-execution anchoring are **POST_MVP** for this implementation scope.
