## Completed milestone

M5's deterministic route/transfer/event schema, accounting translation and PostgreSQL worker preserve one source debit, one unavailable post-burn receivable, at most one destination credit and every observed cost through retries, crashes, reorgs and closure. The finite-yield vault schedule is funded once and frozen before the declared experiment start.

The credentialed gate deployed and finalized fresh vaults on Sepolia and Arc Testnet, performed deposit/redeem round trips, transferred 2 USDC Sepolia→Arc and 1 USDC Arc→Sepolia through CCTP V2 Standard, verified Circle attestations against raw message bytes, finalized both mints, and finalized expected duplicate-mint reverts. Relayer prefunding contributed zero bankroll. Pinned historical reads bind deployment block hashes to token/vault bytecode and decimals.

All 97 raw objects are retained by keccak256 under `.local-evidence/m5` and independently checked by `pnpm test:evidence:m5`. The deterministic [index](evidence/m5-live-index.json) hashes to `0xd425cac12d0d28839cd245fb3f08d90a1993bec39bc22b6dc12fc83369a71527`. Failed earlier attempts remain retained rather than rewritten; only the coherent finalized path is promoted.
